Privacy Policy

OFF EARTH LLC · Last updated July 26, 2026

This policy describes how OFF EARTH LLC ("OFF EARTH", "we") handles your information when you use the OFF EARTH mobile app and offearthllc.com (together, the "Services").

What we collect

  • Account info: email address, username, display name, and an encrypted (hashed) password when you create an account. Optional profile details you choose to add (bio, profile photo, creator identity).
  • Content you post: community posts, comments, likes, and images you upload; design artwork you submit to the Creator Studio.
  • Order info: items purchased, order totals, and the shipping address you provide so we can fulfill orders.
  • Waitlist email: if you join the waitlist on our website.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number is collected and stored by Stripe, never by OFF EARTH — we do not see or store card numbers. Stripe also screens transactions for fraud.

Where your data lives

Account and order data is stored in our database. Images you upload are hosted by Cloudinary. Orders are fulfilled by print-on-demand partners who receive only what's needed to ship your order (items and shipping address).

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't show third-party ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't collect your precise location.

Community content & moderation

Posts you share in the community are visible to other members. You can report any content and block any user in the app; reported content is reviewed and objectionable material is removed within 24 hours.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and associated content at any time in the app: Profile → Danger Zone → Delete My Account. This removes your account, posts, comments, designs, and order history from our systems.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we materially change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above.
